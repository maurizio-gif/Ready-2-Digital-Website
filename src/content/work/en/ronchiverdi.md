## Context

Ronchiverdi had no digital system in place yet: communication ran through WhatsApp and phone calls, credits and renewals were tracked from memory, and complaints left no trace anywhere.

## Challenge

Building a CRM straight away would have meant building on nothing: without knowing how requests actually arrive, who handles them and where they get lost, even a well-built piece of software would have simply replicated on screen the same problems that existed on paper.

## Understanding the process

Before any software component, a real audit: how requests arrive, who handles them, where they get lost, which complaints keep repeating in the same shape. Communication, credit management, renewals, complaints — mapped as they actually worked, not as they were assumed to work.

## Solution

The software built afterwards — a website and a CRM — follows that map, not a standard template for "sports-club software". The point of this case isn't how many components got built: it's that the architecture comes from the process map, not the other way round.

## What we built

- A public website for contact capture and appointment booking, with an automatic reminder ahead of each meeting.
- An internal CRM with a sales pipeline for contacts coming from the website, per-operator permissions, push notifications, and a real-time alert when an opportunity hasn't been picked up by anyone yet.

This project is more recent than the others: we don't yet have enough of a data window to publish a business metric that would mean anything. We'd rather say so than estimate one.
