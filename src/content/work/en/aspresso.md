## Context

Not every process worth digitising lives on a website or in a CRM. For Aspresso, the critical point was administrative: carrying every transaction through to its fiscal receipt, and from there to a figure ready for the books, with no manual step in between.

## Solution

The flow follows the transaction from origin to its accounting entry: transaction → fiscal receipt → compliance record → administrative data → bookkeeping. It's the kind of process people usually only notice when it breaks — the goal here was for it not to be noticed at all.
