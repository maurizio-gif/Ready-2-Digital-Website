## Context

Not every project starts with a website or a CRM to build. For Enjoy Club, a two-location club, the first step was looking at the data the club already had — in its own Airtable CRM — to understand what was actually happening to its leads before proposing any solution at all.

## Understanding the process

The analysis broke down, location by location, how many leads come in, how many get assigned to a sales rep, how many convert, and how long that takes. It turned out that a meaningful share of leads — more than one in ten — never had anyone working them, and that the channels with the highest conversion rate weren't the ones bringing in the most volume.

## What we found

This case shows the method's first step on its own: an audit can stand as an analysis in its own right, when that's what the client actually needs at that moment, instead of automatically turning into a software project. Individual reps' names and case-by-case detail stay internal to the club — what's shown here is the aggregate picture only.
