## Context

Enjoy Club, two locations, runs its website on WordPress and its CRM on
Airtable. Not every business needs a custom system: here the right tools were
already in place, and the job was making them perform as well as they could.

## Challenge

With two locations and several reps working leads in parallel, the risk is
that someone goes unattended without anyone noticing — and that the channel
bringing in the most contacts isn't the one converting the most of them.

## Solution

We worked on the existing Airtable CRM to give the club an honest read of its
own numbers: how many leads come in, how many get assigned, how many convert,
and from which channel. In the same period we also designed the structure to
move tracking of expired renewals onto Airtable, which had previously lived
in separate spreadsheets.

## What we built

- A public website on WordPress.
- A CRM on Airtable managing leads across both locations.
- Ongoing sales-performance analysis on the CRM data, broken down by location
  and by channel.
- A proposed structure for moving expired-renewal tracking onto Airtable.

Individual reps' data stays internal to the club: what's shown here is the
aggregate picture only.
