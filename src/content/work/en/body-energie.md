## Context

Body Energie runs its website and its CRM on Airtable. The need was simple to
state and hard to have without watching it consistently: knowing, week by
week, how many contacts come in, how many genuinely stay in the pipeline, and
how many get lost — not at month's end, but as it happens.

## Solution

Five weekly reports, built on the same Airtable CRM data, followed the same
cohort of contacts over time: how many convert, in how many days, and how
many end up "lost" without a final note explaining why.

## What we built

- A public website and an Airtable CRM managing the centre's leads.
- Weekly sales-funnel reporting, using the same cohort method as Enjoy Club.

## Results

The average time to convert a contact turned out to be longer than the team
expected — a fact that, on its own, changes when you decide to follow up with
someone before writing them off. Like Enjoy Club, individual reps' data stays
internal to the centre: only the aggregate numbers appear here.
