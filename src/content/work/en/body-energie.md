## Context

For Body Energie the need was simple to state and hard to have without a dedicated tool: knowing, week by week, how many contacts come in, how many genuinely stay in the pipeline, and how many get lost — not at month's end, but as it happens.

## Understanding the process

Five weekly reports, built on the client's own CRM data, followed the same cohort of contacts over time: how many convert, in how many days, and how many end up "lost" without a final note explaining why.

## What we found

The average time to convert a contact turned out to be longer than the team expected — a fact that, on its own, changes when you decide to follow up with someone before writing them off. Like Enjoy Club, this case stayed an analysis: individual reps' data stays internal to the centre, and only the aggregate numbers appear here.
