## Context

Athlon Club 4.0 is a sports club in north Rome running since 1973 — swimming, fitness, ice skating. We don't treat it as an ordinary client: it's the business where our method was born, because Ready2Digital's founder is also the person who runs Athlon day to day. Every component built here was lived as an operating problem first, not designed on a whiteboard.

## Challenge

An organisation with over fifty years of history, running on tools that didn't talk to each other: paper enrolments, communication scattered across the front desk, the phone and WhatsApp, no single view of who was a genuine sales opportunity and who was already a settled contact. Every new channel — a website form, an Instagram DM, a phone call — added a piece of information nobody put back together with the rest.

## Solution

No software bought off the shelf. First an audit of the club's real processes — bookings, memberships, access, family accounts — then the choice of tools. The public website generates and qualifies contacts; an internal CRM works them through a sales pipeline and a shared calendar; automation routes and follows up on them; an AI assistant answers in the meantime, reading a verified knowledge base of the club instead of guessing.

## What we built

- A public website with an AI assistant that answers on pricing, schedules and procedures, citing the source for every answer, and hands off to a person when a question falls outside what it knows.
- An internal CRM with a sales pipeline, a shared calendar, a customer record with full history, and automatic lead-to-record matching with a visible confidence score.
- Two-way sync with the club's management software (PerfectGym): no duplicate records, nothing to re-type by hand between the website, the CRM and the front desk.
- Automated email and WhatsApp on leads and renewal dates, plus automatic classification of sales-call notes.
- An on-site check-in kiosk for people arriving without an account, built for the vertical screen at the entrance — not a shrunk-down version of the website.
