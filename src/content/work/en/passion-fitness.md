## Context

Passion Fitness needed a public website that would generate contacts and send people to book and pay directly inside the gym's existing management software, rather than maintaining a second, parallel booking system.

## Solution

The website hands bookings and membership checkout off to the gym's management software (PerfectGym) instead of duplicating them — one single source of truth for who booked what. The "bring a friend" referral programme is automated, and site performance was treated as a requirement, not something to revisit later.

## What we built

- A public website with an editorial blog and class/membership pages.
- A booking calendar and checkout wired into the gym's management software.
- A "free trial" form and automation of the referral programme.
- Image and load-time optimisation: the homepage dropped from 45.49 MB to 4.93 MB on first load — roughly a tenth of its original weight.
